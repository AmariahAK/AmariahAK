## 🌌 Flagship Project: Atlarix
**The Premium Desktop AI Copilot**
*Currently in the forge. Shipping fast. Landing page pending.*

> **Note:** I'm prioritizing the engine over the showroom. You can see the weekly progress and raw build logs [here on LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/amariah-kamau-3156412a6/).

Atlarix is my current mission—a powerful desktop application designed to give developers a tool-augmented workflow without sacrificing privacy or control.

* **🔐 Privacy-First & BYOK:** Complete control over your intelligence. Connect to local models via **Ollama** or **LMStudio**, or tap into 100+ cloud models through **OpenRouter**.
* **🛠️ Agentic Tool System:** A growing suite of 55+ tools that let the AI act—not just suggest—inside your workspace.
* **📊 AI Diagram System:** Automatically generates and maintains interactive architecture diagrams by analyzing your actual codebase. 
* **🚀 Smart Scaffolding:** A CLI-first approach to project creation. It executes commands like `npx create-vite` to build your foundation correctly.
* **🎯 Surgical Precision:** Targeted editing tools (`replace_code`, `insert_code`) that respect your existing code style and minimize token waste.
